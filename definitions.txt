Simulation and Validation of a Driven Two-Level Quantum System

The physics

Consider a system with Hamiltonian
 H = \omega / 2 \sigma_z + \Omega \sigma_x

 where \omega = energy splitting, \Omega = driving term, 
 \sigma_x \sigma_z = Pauli matrices

 We want to simulate the evolution:
 |psi(t)> = exp(-iHt) |psi(0)>
